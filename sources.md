## D. Court / regulator counter-evidence (referenced in `court-counter-evidence.md`)

1. **People v. Grayson** (N.Y. App. Div.) — indictments are not negotiable
   instruments.
2. **United States v. Cotto** (1st Cir.) — indictments are not negotiable
   instruments.
3. **Ratzlaf v. United States** (U.S. Supreme Court) — no commercial intent
   requirement in structuring statutes.
4. **Commonwealth v. Swisher** (Mass.) — criminal proceedings do not proceed
   under the UCC.
5. **Treasury Bureau of the Fiscal Service** — Admitted Reinsurers list under
   Department Circular 570.
