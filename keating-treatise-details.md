# Jean Keating - "Prison Treatise" - Details

This file fixes the who/what/when/where of the document and the seminar series
Keating uses as the source of the "prison bonds" / "statute law is contracts"
theory.

## The document

- **Title:** Jean Keating's Prison Treatise
- **Dated:** November 30, 2004 (the treatise itself). A companion seminar was
  given **October 10, 2004**, and is referred to as "Jean Keating I, II, III".
- **Format:** Long-form written treatise (reported across multiple mirrors as
  roughly 23-36 pages) plus a workshop transcript (transcribed by Rockney

- **Mirrors:** educrated in law, You Are Law, PDFCOFFEE, Scribd, Yumpu,

- **"Jean Keating Securitization 101"** and **"Jean Keating and Jack Smith"** —

## The core theory

1. The courts operate under **Statute Law** (Law Merchant / UCC), not common law.
2. "Statutes" are legally defined as bonds or obligations of record (Black's
   Law Dictionary, 4th ed.).
3. The criminal complaint/information/indictment is a **three-party Bill of
   Exchange / Draft under UCC Article 3**. The Grand Jury Foreman is the
   Drawer/Maker; the defendant is the Drawee; the State is the Payee; the
   "live man" (victim) is the Payor. The court seeks an "acceptor" under
   **UCC 3-410**.
4. Every criminal case is accompanied by **three GSA standard forms**:
   - **SF24** (Bid Bond) — state courts
   - **SF25** (Performance Bond)
   - **SF25A** (Payment Bond)
   Federal courts use **SF273, SF274, SF275** respectively.
5. The clerk issues the "Bid Bond" when a case is lodged; the bond sets the
   value of the debt the state will collect.
6. **Prisons are "repository institutions"** — prisoners are assets/collateral
   for the bid, performance, and payment bonds, functioning like a depository
   bank under **17 CFR 450**.
7. Prisoners are themselves the **reinsured/surety**; the sentence is the
   "valued and marketable risk."
8. **"All crimes are commercial"** per **27 CFR 72.11**.
9. Individuals can use **Exemption Priority** (Uniform Exemption Act) to
   discharge "Public Debt" on the "Private side of the accounting cycle."

## The open question Keating puts to the reader

> "Why aren't we as principals on the Private side of the accounting cycle
> using our Exemption Priority to discharge all this Public Debt?"

## Recognized limitations of the source

- The treatise is a **persuasive/forensic document**, not a neutral legal
  analysis.
- It relies heavily on commercial-law vocabulary applied outside its
  commercial context (courts, criminal charges, prisons).
- The seminar transcript is a **first-person record**, not an objective
  ledger. It reflects Keating's and his interlocutors' claims, not verified
  fact.
- Nothing in it is peer-reviewed, and it is not published by a law school,
  bar association, or government body.
