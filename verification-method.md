# Verification Method

This repository is a fact-check. Every claim can be independently
re-verified. Here's how.

## 0. Golden rule
If a primary source is paywalled or not publicly accessible, mark it
**UNVERIFIED** instead of inferring. Do not let a quote from the treatise
stand in for a primary document.

## 1. Verify a phone number
1. Open the relevant SEC EDGAR filing or government registry.
2. Search for the exact number, address, or contact name.
3. Confirm it appears verbatim.
4. If it does not appear in the primary document, mark **UNVERIFIED**.

Example: National City Bank's phone (216) 222-2552 and halo Pattison as Transfer Agent
appear in SEC EDGAR forms. The claim stands only if the primary filing confirms it.

## 2. Verify a URL / website / treatise citation
1. Open the canonical URL.
2. Note whether it resolves, its date, and its content.
3. If it 404s, find the mirror (Educated in Law, You Are Law, PDFCOFFEE,
   Scribd, etc.) and record that the mirror exists but the canonical URL is
   broken.
4. Record the date you checked it.

## 3. Verify a statute or regulation
1. Open the official codified text (e.g., `uscode.house.gov`, `govinfo.gov`,
   the Federal Register, or a government bureau's site).
2. Search for the section number and read the actual text.
3. Check whether the treatise's description matches the text.
4. Record your finding.

Example: 27 CFR 72.11 is part of Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco, Firearms, and
Explosives). The treatise says it claims "all crimes are commercial." Read
the text. It is about ATF licensing, not criminal-commercial classification.

## 4. Verify a company or organization
1. Go to a primary, authoritative registry (SEC EDGAR, a state business
registry, a government agency site, or the company's official site).
2. Confirm the name, address, phone, and contact person.
3. If the trademark is a registered product/pattern (e.g., "BRAND NAME")
used in the treatise, record whether it is a real trademark and where.

Example: National City Bank (Cleveland) is a real bank. The phone and transfer
agent details are verifiable on SEC EDGAR. Do not treat their phone number as
marketing evidence for a conspiracy claim.

## 5. Verify a court claim
1. Look up the docket on a court system (PACER, a state court site, or a
  court's public opinions database).
2. Read the opinion.
3. Record what the court actually held.

Example: courts uniformly reject "indictment = negotiable instrument" and
"UCC governs criminal charges." Confirm the current docket and holding.

## 6. Mark a claim as
- **VERIFIED** — the primary source confirms it exactly.
- **PARTIALLY VERIFIED** — part matches, part is inaccurate.
- **UNVERIFIED** — not enough public evidence; do not infer.
- **FALSE** — the primary source contradicts it.

## 7. Record your work
For every claim in this repo, note:
- The claim
- The source it cites
- The primary source you checked
- What that primary source says
- Your verdict (verified / partially verified / unverified / false)

## 8. What this repo is not
- Not a legal brief.
- Not a sovereign-citizen / redemptionist defense.
- Not a claim that any court opinion in this repo is the "correct" law.
- Not a record of your personal beliefs or your intent.

This repo is a tool to keep the record straight. Use it that way.
