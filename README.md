# Jean Keating — Prison Treatise (Fact-Check Repository)

This repository holds a structured, evidence-first fact-check of **Jean Keating's
"Prison Treatise" (November 30, 2004)** and the companion seminar tapes
(*Jean Keating I, II, III*, October 2004). The thesis of the treatise and the
seminars is that the modern U.S. criminal court system operates as a commercial
debt-collection apparatus, that "statutes are bonds," that an indictment is a
negotiable instrument (UCC Article 3), and that prisoners are effectively the
asset/reinsurance underlying the bid, performance, and payment bonds that fund
the prison system.

This repo does **not** advance the treatise's claims. It maps them to their
primary sources and to the case law and regulatory documents that courts have
used to reject them.

## Concepts covered (and where they stand)

- **Statutes as "bonds/obligations of record"** — dictionary fact; courts apply
  statutes as enacted, not as commercial bonds.
- **Indictment = negotiable instrument (UCC 3-410)** — courts reject this.
  An indictment is a court-issued charging document, not a draft/note/payable.
- **Prisons = depository banks (17 CFR 450)** — the SEC definition does not
  classify prisons as depository banks.
- **Prisoners = reinsurance/surety assets** — creative theory; no regulatory
  framework treats prisons as insurance vehicles.
- **GSA SF24/SF25/SF25A = "prison bonds"** — real GSA forms, but they are
  *construction/procurement* surety bonds under the FAR, not criminal charging
  instruments.
- **27 CFR 72.11 = "all crimes are commercial"** — that section is about
  liquor (alcohol manufacturing/importation), not a general criminal-commercial
  framework.
- **Exemption Priority / "Uniform Exemption Act § 3"** — concerns exempting
  property from execution/lien, not automatic debt discharge.
- **11th Circuit navigation to Treasury "List of Sureties" (FMS.Treas.gov)** —
  the Treasury "Admitted Reinsurer" concept is real (Department Circular 570),
  but the claim that the 11th Circuit *hosts* these pages is not established.

## How to use this repo

1. Read `claim-by-claim-factcheck.md` first.
2. Pick a claim you want to verify.
3. Follow `verification-method.md` — it points to the exact public source to open.
4. Everything here is publicly verifiable; nothing is hidden.

## Important caveat

This is a fact-check repository, **not** a legal brief and **not** a defense of
the sovereign-citizen/redemptionist legal theory the treatise promotes. Courts
have repeatedly rejected the specific theories above, and this repo records that,
not to argue with you, but to keep the record honest.
