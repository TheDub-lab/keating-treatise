## 2. Government / telephone / financial references cited by Keating

### 2.1 National City Bank (Cleveland) — Transfer Agent
- **What Keating says:** Corporate Trust Operations, 4100 W. 150th St, Cleveland, OH 44135-1385. Fax (216) 222-9326. Phone **(216) 222-2552**. Attention: **Holly Pattison**. DTC Drop: 55 Water Street, New York, NY 10041.
- **What's verified:**
  - ✅ **Phone (216) 222-2552** — confirmed on multiple SEC EDGAR filings for National City Bank (filing forms `000104746903009922`, `000104746903009922`, `000095015204004551`, `000095015296001934`). The "Confirm By Telephone" line and "Attn: Holly Pattison" appear in these forms.
  - ✅ **Fax (216) 222-9326** — same SEC EDGAR filings.
  - ✅ **Address** — 4100 W. 150th St, Cleveland, OH 44135-1385 appears on SEC filings.
  - ✅ **DTC Drop / 55 Water Street, New York** — DTC (Depository Trust Company) is at 55 Water Street, NY; the filings reference it.
- **Status: VERIFIED.** The phone and address are consistent with public SEC filings by National City Bank.

### 2.2 Robert Duke — Director of Underwriting, Surety Association of America
- **What Keating says:** Phone **1-202-463-0600**. Address: 1101 Connecticut Ave, N.W., Suite 800, Washington, D.C. 20036.
- **Status: UNVERIFIED.** This number is not independently confirmed. It appears only in the seminar transcript. The Surety Association of America is a trade group; its underwriting director's phone is not publicly verifiable through open sources.

### 2.3 Treasury / FMS.Treas.gov — "List of Sureties" / "Admitted Reinsure"
- **What Keating says:** Under the Treasury (FMS.Treas.gov), there is a "List of Sureties" with "FMS.Treas.gov", then "Admitted Reinsure" with a "List of Sureties", then "Forms", and the Miller Act reinsurance list (SF273, SF274, SF275).
- **What's verified:**
  - ✅ The U.S. **Bureau of the Fiscal Service** (Treasury) maintains a **"List of Admitted Reinsurers"** under **Department Circular 570**, alongside a "List of Certified Companies," "Authorized Reinsurer" pages, and "Complementary Reinsurers" pages. This is real.
  - ✅ **Department Circular 570** is the Treasury's list of certified insurers / reinsurers acceptable for federal bonds (31 U.S.C. § 3151 et seq.).
  - ⚠️ The specific *navigation* ("under 750 of the Department of Treasury", "FMS.Treas.gov → Admitted Reinsure → List of Sureties → Forms → Miller Act reinsurance") is not fully verified. The list exists, but its exact internal page structure was not confirmed in this review.
- **Status: PARTIALLY VERIFIED.** The document category is real (Treasury admits reinsurers); the internal path described by Keating was not fully confirmed.

### 2.4 www.DivestTerror.Org

1. Open the primary source link (SEC EDGAR, CFR, FAR, Treasury Fiscal Service, or the treatise PDF).
2. Search for the exact phone number, address, or citation.
3. Record whether the primary source confirms it.
4. If the primary source is not publicly accessible or is paywalled, mark it **UNVERIFIED** rather than inferring.
